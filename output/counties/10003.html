1.
Strong labor market fundamentals. Average wage of $77,977/yr with 19.2% per capita income growth over 4 years.
2.
Housing market has sustained momentum. FHFA 3-year average appreciation of +6.3%/yr with 1,322 units permitted — demand signal is consistent.
3.
Quality of place is above average. Favorable urban access, amenity density, and crime rate versus peer counties.
4.
Low natural hazard exposure. Flood, storm, and wildfire risk all below national median — insurance costs are likely at or below national average.
⚠️ Bear Case — What Could Go Wrong
1.
Net out-migration signal. IRS data shows more households leaving than arriving. Long-term demand trajectory warrants monitoring.
2.
Stretched valuation. P/R of 16.8x and breakeven horizon of 9.3 years require a long hold to justify ownership over renting.
3.
Rate sensitivity. Stress-test your monthly budget at 8.5% before committing. A 150bps rate increase adds roughly $305/mo to carrying cost.
