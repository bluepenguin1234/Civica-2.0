The Problem

Home search tools tell you the
price. Nobody tells you if the
county works.

You're about to make the biggest financial decision of your life. Your agent doesn't know if the local economy is growing. Zillow doesn't show you who's moving in. Nobody tells you the real climate risk — until after you close.

🏛️
Is the local economy getting stronger or weaker?
Wage growth, sector quality, income growth, economic diversification — none of this appears on a listing. A county where workers are getting richer is a fundamentally different bet than one where they're leaving.
📈
Is the housing market peaking or just starting?
FHFA 3-year appreciation trends, inventory tightness, and the permit pipeline tell you whether a market is accelerating, topping, or rolling over. Past appreciation is not a buy signal — trajectory is.
🔥
What's the real climate and insurance risk?
Flood claims, storm damage per capita, and wildfire exposure are already driving insurance crises in hundreds of counties. Civica quantifies the hazard before you sign — not after your first renewal.
