Methodology Page — Dimension Weights & Market Signals Accordion
25 PTS
Dimension 1: Affordability & Value
Zillow ZHVI + HUD FMR FY2026 + BEA CAINC1 + FHFA HPI
The largest dimension (25% of total score). Measures whether the price is defensible: P/R ratio, price-to-income ratio, buy-vs-rent breakeven, and appreciation quality. Every formula is derived from federal data.
22 PTS
Dimension 2: Economic Vitality
BLS QCEW 2023 + BEA CAINC1
Average wage level, employment sector quality score (NAICS-weighted), economic diversification (Herfindahl Index), and 4-year per capita income growth. All from the Bureau of Labor Statistics and Bureau of Economic Analysis.
20 PTS
Dimension 3: Housing Market Dynamics
FHFA HPI (county) + Zillow inventory + Census BPS
Two FHFA price signals (3-year trend + current momentum), supply tightness from Zillow active listings, and new housing permit pipeline from Census Building Permits Survey. Supply and demand together.
15 PTS
Dimension 4: Quality of Place
FBI NIBRS 2024 + USDA RUCC 2023 + Census CBP 2023
Violent crime rate (FBI National Master File, 21,068 agencies), urban access (USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Code 1–9 scale), and amenity density (private establishments per 1,000 residents from Census County Business Patterns).
12 PTS
Dimension 5: Physical Risk
FEMA NFIP + NOAA Storm Events + USFS Wildfire Risk to Communities
Three natural hazard proxies: flood insurance claims per capita (10-year window), storm property damage per capita (5-year window), and USFS wildfire national risk rank. These are the same underlying datasets FEMA uses to build its National Risk Index.
6 PTS
Dimension 6: Population Momentum
Census Population Estimates 2025 + IRS SOI Migration 2022-23
Net migration rate (Census RNETMIG2023: arrivals minus departures per 1,000 residents) and in-mover income quality (IRS AGI data: do arriving households earn more than departing ones?). 60/40 weighted split.

DISCLOSED
Known Limitations — Fully Disclosed
METHODOLOGY.md §14
Property tax hardcoded at 1.2% (actual range: 0.28%–2.23%). Inventory is raw listing count, not months of supply. P/I uses per-capita income (BEA), not median household income. HPI signals are correlated, not fully independent. All 14 limitations are publicly documented — competitors hide theirs.

Lake County, IL — County Report Hero
Lake County, IL — Full Report (Valuation, Economic Fundamentals, Supply & Demand)
$2,629
Monthly Ownership Cost (PITI)
Zillow ZHVI ($390,184) + HUD FMR rate assumptions
Principal & Interest + Property Tax + Insurance. Formula: $390,184 × 80% loan at 7% 30yr = ~$2,076 P&I + 1.2% property tax ($390/yr) + 0.5% insurance ($163/yr) = $2,629/mo. This is the monthly "cost to own" number that drives the breakeven calculation.
$390,184
Median Home Value
Zillow ZHVI — County-level Home Value Index (latest month)
Zillow ZHVI is the only non-federal data source in Civica's model. It is used here because no federal dataset provides county-level median home values at monthly granularity. FHFA HPI is used for all price-change signals — Zillow only for the dollar-level anchor.
+7.7%/yr
3-Year Avg Annual Appreciation
FHFA House Price Index — County-Level, 3-Year Average
The Federal Housing Finance Agency publishes quarterly HPI for ~2,800 counties. Civica computes the average annual change over the most recent 3 years. Above 7% triggers a "froth risk" flag — sustained appreciation above this range historically precedes cooling. Lake County at 7.7% is right at that threshold.
7.7 yrs
Buy vs. Rent Breakeven Horizon
Civica derived metric — Zillow + HUD FMR FY2026
The breakeven answers: "How many years must I hold before owning beats renting?" Formula: 20% down payment ÷ (monthly PITI − monthly rent) ÷ 12. HUD Fair Market Rent (2BR) = $1,781/mo. PITI = $2,629/mo. Monthly ownership premium = $848. Down payment = $78,037. Breakeven ≈ $78,037 ÷ ($848 × 12 × appreciation adjustment) = 7.7 years. Above 7 years signals caution.
-3,884 HH
Net Household Migration
IRS Statistics of Income — Migration Data 2022-23
The IRS tracks every household that files a tax return with a different address year-over-year. 296,584 households moved IN; 300,468 moved OUT. Net: -3,884. This is a red flag for Lake County — more households are leaving than arriving. The 1.01x income ratio (in-movers earn 1% more than out-movers) partially offsets the concern.
18.3x
Price-to-Rent Ratio
Zillow ZHVI ÷ (HUD FMR 2BR × 12)
$390,184 ÷ ($1,781 × 12) = $390,184 ÷ $21,372 = 18.3x. The national norm is 15–18x. At 18.3x, Lake County sits at the upper edge of normal — not stretched, but not a deep-value buy on P/R alone. Markets below 15x are historically undervalued; above 20x suggests renters are getting a better deal.
94/100
Economic Vitality — Near Top Decile
BLS QCEW 2023 + BEA CAINC1
Average annual wage: $93,318 (above national median). Per capita income growth: +27.5% over 4 years. Sector quality score: 1.04 (above-neutral — mix of professional/finance/healthcare). HHI concentration index: 1,026 (well-diversified — no single sector dominates). This is the dimension driving Lake County's #1 ranking.
610
Violent Crime Rate (per 100k residents)
FBI NIBRS 2024 National Master File — 21,068 agencies
4,323 violent offenses reported across Lake County agencies. 610 per 100k vs. national estimate of ~380. This is the primary drag on Lake County's Quality of Place score (80/100 — still good, but the crime rate is above average). FBI NIBRS covers UCR Part 1 offenses only: homicide, rape, robbery, aggravated assault — not simple assault or intimidation.
✅
Why Lake County ranks #1: It combines a near-top-decile economy (avg wage $93k, diversified employment, strong income growth) with manageable affordability (18.3x P/R — at the edge of normal but not stretched), low physical risk, and above-average quality of place. The net out-migration is a known watch item, but the economic fundamentals are strong enough to overcome it in the composite score. A buyer with a 10+ year horizon in a high-wage job sees this as a compelling market.

The Data Foundation
14 Datasets · 13 Federal Sources · $0 Cost
Every number on every county page traces back to one of these 14 datasets. The model is fully reproducible — anyone with Python and an internet connection can download these files and verify every score.
Dataset Agency Used For Coverage
Zillow ZHVI Zillow (only non-federal source) Dim 1: median home value, P/R ratio; Dim 3: active inventory count ~2,900 counties, monthly
FHFA House Price Index Federal Housing Finance Agency Dim 1: appreciation quality; Dim 3: 3-yr trend + current momentum ~2,800 counties, quarterly
HUD Fair Market Rents FY2026 Dept. of Housing & Urban Development Dim 1: rent baseline for P/R ratio and buy-vs-rent breakeven All US counties, annual
BEA CAINC1 (Local Area Income) Bureau of Economic Analysis Dim 1: price-to-income ratio; Dim 2: 4-year income growth rate All US counties, annual
BLS QCEW (Quarterly Census of Employment & Wages) Bureau of Labor Statistics Dim 2: avg annual wage, sector quality score, HHI diversification index All US counties, annual
Census Building Permits Survey US Census Bureau Dim 3: new housing supply pipeline (units permitted 2022) All US counties, annual
Census County Business Patterns US Census Bureau Dim 4: amenity density (private establishments per 1,000 residents) All US counties, annual
USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes USDA Economic Research Service Dim 4: urban access score (1 = large metro, 9 = most rural) All US counties, every 5-10 yrs
FBI NIBRS 2024 National Master File Federal Bureau of Investigation Dim 4: violent crime rate per 100k residents (UCR Part 1 offenses only) 21,068 agencies, 49 states, 2,869 counties
FEMA NFIP Flood Insurance Claims Federal Emergency Management Agency Dim 5: paid flood claims per capita, 10-year window All insured counties
NOAA Storm Events Database National Oceanic & Atmospheric Administration Dim 5: property damage per capita from storm events, 5-year window All US counties, 2019–2023
USFS Wildfire Risk to Communities US Forest Service Dim 5: wildfire national risk rank (exposure index) All US counties
Census Population Estimates 2025 US Census Bureau Base county universe, migration rate denominators, per-capita calculations All US counties, annual
IRS Statistics of Income — Migration Data Internal Revenue Service Dim 6: net migration rate (via Census); in-mover income quality ratio (AGI basis) All US counties, 2022-23
🔒
Why federal data creates the trust moat: Every federal dataset is (1) publicly available at no cost, (2) collected by agencies with no financial stake in real estate outcomes, (3) methodologically consistent across all 3,143 US counties, and (4) updated on regular government publication schedules. A real estate platform built on agent data or proprietary indices can be influenced. A platform built on IRS tax filings, FBI crime statistics, and FHFA price indices cannot.
